# MHOP Songs 🎵

**A multilingual Christian worship songbook for Android.**

MHOP Songs is a modern, minimalist Android application built to bring Telugu, Hindi and English Christian worship songs together in one accessible digital library.

Designed with simplicity and readability in mind, MHOP Songs provides a comfortable digital songbook experience for church communities, worship teams and individuals.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Multilingual Library** – Browse Christian songs in Telugu, Hindi and English.
- **Song Reader** – Enjoy a clean, distraction-free lyrics reading experience.
- **Customizable Typography** – Adjust font size, font family, style, weight and line spacing.
- **Chords & Musical Details** – View song chords, musical keys and related information.
- **Smart Document Import** – Import songs from DOCX and PDF documents with automatic extraction.
- **Song Management** – Add, edit, organize and delete songs through administrator access.
- **Source Links & Credits** – Manage YouTube videos, audio links, lyricists, composers and other credits.
- **Favourites** – Save frequently used songs for quick access.
- **Offline Access** – Read previously synchronized songs without an internet connection.
- **Cloud Synchronization** – Share the song library across devices using Firebase.
- **Light & Dark Themes** – Enjoy a comfortable reading experience in different lighting conditions.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

MHOP Songs follows a minimalist design approach inspired by its official brand identity.

- Blue, white and black brand palette.
- 60/30/10 color distribution.
- Comfortable typography and generous whitespace.
- Simple navigation and accessible controls.
- A distraction-free reading experience.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Design System:** Material 3
- **Architecture:** MVVM
- **Local Storage:** Room
- **Backend:** Firebase
